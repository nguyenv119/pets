Overnight Rosemary Focaccia

By Ines Marlowe · 12 min read · Serves 8

Mix it after dinner, forget about it, and bake it for lunch. A long, cold rest does the kneading for you.

Method

  1. Stir 500 g bread flour, 400 g cool water, 10 g salt and 3 g yeast until no dry flour remains.
  2. Fold the dough over itself four times. Repeat twice more, 30 minutes apart.
  3. Turn it in 40 g olive oil in a 23 x 33 cm tin and refrigerate 12 to 18 hours.
  4. Warm for 2 hours, dimple deeply, then bake at 230 °C for 22 to 25 minutes.

Dimple all the way to the tin; shallow dents fill back in.

At a glance

  • Hands-on 25 MIN
  • Cold rest 12-18 H
  • Oven 230°C
